The first build of Althar for testers, on Macs with Apple silicon.

- Open a folder as a project and tell its coordinator what you want changed. It plans tasks, each with a lead that implements it and a review.
- Claude Code, Codex and OpenCode, with the accounts you already signed in with on this Mac.
- The home shows what needs you across every project; permissions are answered there or in the task.
- Dictation runs on this Mac, after a one-time download of the speech model.
- Althar updates itself. When a new version is ready, a line at the top of the window says so; it installs when you restart Althar from that line or quit it, never while work runs.

Tell us what breaks: https://github.com/AltharIDE/althar/issues
