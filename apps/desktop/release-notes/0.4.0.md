What changed since 0.3.0.

## New

- Report a problem, from Help in the app menu, the foot of quick settings, About Althar in Settings, or a stuck step. It sends what you write, with a screenshot and Althar's logs, with folders, names, email addresses and keys taken out. Either can be switched off, and See what's sent lists everything.
- Althar keeps its own logs for about a week, in ~/Library/Logs/Althar on a Mac. They never hold your prompts, messages or files.
- Dictation keeps going when you leave its screen. A pill says who the message is for; from it you can stop, throw it away, or go back to its composer.
- Push and merge from a board card, without opening the task.
- Tasks waiting for a free agent are listed in Up next, in the order they will start.
- A merged task with commits after its pull request can leave them out and settle.

## Improved

- Between sending and the first word, Thinking shows where the reply will start, with the seconds once it takes a while.
- Stuck cards say in plain words what happened and which button to press.
- The Run button gives a long command a short name, with the whole command on hover.
- The composer's foot stays on one row and nothing is cut off in a narrow window; Send stays in reach while you dictate.
- The model list cuts long names and keeps their tags on one line.
- Tasks and cards no longer carry the Unrestricted label. The switch is unchanged.

## Fixed

- Abandoning a task closes its pull request on GitHub, GitLab or Bitbucket, and its branch, instead of only saying so. A settled task says how its pull request stands.
- Send back starts the lead again when its session has ended, instead of leaving the task in Needs you.
- The home and the board agree on what needs you: a finished task no longer counts, and a task with two questions is one line.
- A local merge no longer fails on a submodule it doesn't move. When one is in the way, it names it and shows git's whole message.
- The board's lanes start right under the project header.

Tell us what breaks: Report a problem in the app, or https://github.com/AltharIDE/althar/issues
