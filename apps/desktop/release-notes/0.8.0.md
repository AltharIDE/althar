What changed since 0.7.0.

A browser built in: a task's app opens beside its conversation, you comment on what you see, and the lead clicks through the same page to check its work. Leads also draw on canvases, ⌘K searches everything, tasks keep going after they're done and wait for checks or a merge by themselves, small changes land in one step, and agents ask only before what can't be undone.

## New

- **A browser beside the conversation.** A task's app, and any link in a thread, open beside the conversation, in a browser session of the task's own.
- **Comment on the page.** Point at anything on the page and write a comment. It goes to the lead with your next message, with a picture of the spot.
- **Leads use the page.** The lead clicks, types, scrolls and takes screenshots in the same page you're looking at, to check what it built. A click of yours stops it.
- **Canvases.** Leads and the coordinator draw pages, mockups, diagrams and charts beside the conversation. Comment on one, and the next version answers it.
- **Artifacts.** Every canvas a project's agents drew, in one view of the project, with each version.
- **Search everything.** ⌘K finds tasks, pull requests, issues, what was said, notes and files, and acts from there: allow, merge, stop, open on the host, or ask the coordinator.
- **Keep talking to a finished task.** Message its lead once its plan is done. What it commits waits for Ship, which reviews it and pushes it to the pull request.
- **Tasks wait, then carry on.** A lead can wait for checks to pass, a pull request to merge or a set time, and carries on by itself when it happens, without holding an agent meanwhile.
- **Helpers.** A lead can start other agents that work in the background while you keep talking to it.
- **Several changes in one task,** each with its own branch and pull request, side by side or stacked.
- **Change anything about a task, at any time.** Tell its lead or the coordinator, and its team, its review or its changes change, either way, even once it's running.
- **Small changes, fast.** A change of a few lines takes one step on a quick model, with no countdown, and is reviewed after it merges.
- **Asides.** Quick questions beside the coordinator's conversation, each as a pill over it. One that changes your code becomes a task.
- **Out of usage, carried on.** A step whose agent runs out of usage moves to the strongest model you have with usage left.
- **GitHub Copilot.** Sign in to Copilot and its models run tasks, through OpenCode.
- **Leads start tasks,** which show live in their thread.
- **Leads and the coordinator reach more of each service:** whole issue discussions, comments, attachments and links on every tracker, issues in bulk, Linear's projects and documents, and other tasks' work.
- **Find your way in a long chat.** A mark for each message runs beside the conversation; hover one to preview it, click to jump there.
- **Settings, rebuilt.** Sections in three groups. MCP servers and skills are tiles: add a server or a skill once, and every agent gets it.
- **Reorder Up next by dragging.** The order you set is the order tasks get an agent.
- **A task's card appears as it's drafted.** The plan card fills in while the coordinator writes it, and you can hold it before it's done.
- **Keep a task's setup as a preset.** A task you set up your own way offers to keep its team and steps as a preset for next time.
- **Changes across several repositories** show as one track, merged in order.
- **Merges in one card.** The merges the coordinator is doing show as one card that updates as each lands.
- **Tasks toward another branch.** A task can work toward a branch other than the default one.
- **What a pull request needs before it can merge,** on its card: approvals, checks, an up-to-date branch.
- **Automations run on any agent** you're signed in to, not only on a plan. A run that can't start says why.
- **Before and after screenshots, and GIF recordings,** on pull requests.
- **Review after merge, your way.** A small change's review brings back only major findings by default; a project can bring back every finding, or skip the review.
- **Where a turn's time went.** A turn says how long it waited for a place among the agents or for its session to start, beside the agent's own work.
- **Sign-in warnings.** Althar warns a few days before an agent's sign-in ends.
- **Dictation in Settings,** with your microphone and the speech model.
- **Window controls that match your desktop** on GNOME, KDE and Windows.
- **Close Home.** It stays closed until you open it again.

## Improved

- **Agents ask only before what can't be undone:** merging, publishing, posting as you, deleting. Everything else, the network included, just runs.
- **Althar stays responsive when your computer is busy.** Agents run at a lower priority, and the board, a task and Ship answer quickly under load.
- **A calmer app.** Errors read as errors and say what fixes them, with the fix beside them; the board and Settings say less.
- **Steadier tasks.** Stopped and interrupted work picks up where it was, across restarts, and Ship never pushes what wasn't reviewed.
- **Asides open at once** and don't wait behind tasks for an agent.
- **Hold a queued task, start a held one, or change a task's team** from the board, at any time.
- **Threads read cleaner:** a turn's work is one live line, then folds; thinking folds away; streams are smooth.
- **Threads load earlier turns** as you scroll up.
- **Tools are named in plain words** everywhere: threads, permission cards and notices.
- **The coordinator plans from presets.** The Prototype preset has no review, and presets end a task the way the project says.
- **A skipped review can be added back** until the change merges.
- **Leads retitle and redescribe their own pull requests,** and follow-up pull requests are named for their fix.
- **Titles keep up:** a task and its pull request are renamed when what the task does changes.
- **Board cards** read plainly, and show pictures and files handed back beside their pull request.
- **Models are named as you know them,** never by their raw id.
- **Your own Codex install** runs Codex's sessions, where you have one.
- **Skills are yours.** Althar no longer ships skills of its own; the skills you have reach every agent.
- **Light theme** meets WCAG AA contrast.
- **Faster start.**
- **Flatpak:** git over SSH works, with your ssh-agent.
- Claude Code's adapter is 0.106.0 and Codex's is 2.6.0.

## Fixed

- Messages sent during a turn arrive in the order you sent them.
- Any reply from you clears "Waiting on you".
- A fresh usage reading clears a stale "Out of usage".
- An agent's models show in the picker once you've signed in, without restarting Althar.
- Helpers a restart interrupted carry on after it.
- A lead waiting on your answers is never shown as done.
- Fixing a review after a merge never shows the task as Done or Stopped.
- Comments by a repository's maintainers reach the lead.
- A pull request with more than 100 comments, reviews or checks is read in full.
- Skipped checks no longer count as running or failed.
- A task's screenshots are kept when its change is amended.
- An automation run that can't start shows as blocked, not running.
- A task never starts on a model that isn't available.
- Merging main into a task's branch no longer starts a review round.
- A GitLab merge request opens right after its branch is pushed.
- A remote added after the project was made is picked up.
- The board keeps up with tasks as the task view does.
- Try again after a lost connection always does something.
- Clearing the coordinator's chat clears it.
- A drafted task no longer flashes in Needs you before its plan.
- A finished task moves into Settled once, not again on every reload.
- Enter waits for a voice note to finish before sending.
- Opening a task brings forward the window you last used.
- Dragging the divider between the conversation and the board no longer lags.
