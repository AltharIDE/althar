What changed since 0.2.0.

- A project can run many things, not one. The Run button is split: a press runs the first of the list, and its list starts or stops any of them, several at once. Choose Edit the list… to drag them into order.
- Settings is a tab beside Home, kept on the section you left. The gear at the end of the bar, on every screen, opens quick settings: Keep awake, Notifications, Sound, CPU and memory, and agents at once.
- Allow everything with one switch, for a project in Rules or for a single task from its menu. A task that does shows Unrestricted. Never still holds.
- A task's thread reads in rounds: your ask, its lead's first word, its work in one line you can open, and its result.
- A ready change says what stands in the way of merging (commits to push, a conflict, which check failed) and the next thing to do.
- Failed checks are run again once and read in full before the lead is told; Althar sees a change's checks through until you've looked at it.
- A finished task can go back to work and ship again, as a further pull request.
- Quick tasks stay on this Mac. Ask the lead to make one a change when you want a pull request.
- The coordinator can find and list pull requests, and start a review of someone else's: its lead reads a copy of the code, drafts the review, and you edit, post or discard it.
- ⌘K finds any task, in a project or across all of them.
- Compact sits in the context ring's detail, and a task's lead starts afresh before its context grows large.
- A pasted link's card sits inside your message.
- What you've written in a composer, and dictation, is kept when you go to another screen.
- Permission cards say a long command once, short, with the whole in the thread.
- Download gives the installer for your computer. Anonymous usage data is on by default; turn it off in Settings.
- Fixed: tasks that aren't changes open on their conversation; a failed command keeps what it printed; ordinary edits to one file are no longer stopped as a loop; add-account sign-in for Claude Code works in the Flatpak, and the Linux tray icon is back; a waiting card no longer shows steps or a clock before it starts.

Tell us what breaks: https://github.com/AltharIDE/althar/issues
