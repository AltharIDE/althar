What changed since 0.9.0.

Althar runs on your own machines: in early access, add Linux machines of yours as workers, and your tasks' agents run there, setup, tests and dev servers too, with their commits coming back to your Mac. Your own host, experimental, keeps your projects and their agents working on a server of yours with your laptop closed. Agents can use the apps on your Mac, designs open beside their own chat, and `/goal` keeps an agent working until it's done.

## New

- **Your own workers, in early access.** Add a Linux machine of yours as a worker from Settings › Computers, over SSH or with a one-time code: Althar installs it, signs its agents in and checks it's ready. A task that can run on a worker goes there, or to Any free worker, and moves between machines later. Its setup, builds, tests and dev servers run there, its dev server's page opens in Althar, and its commits come back to where the project lives. For now, workers run on 64-bit Linux with systemd, a task needs a worktree of its own and an agent the worker can run, and reviews stay where the project lives.
- **Your own host, experimental.** Run Althar on a Linux server of yours, so your projects and their agents keep working with your laptop closed, and your workers can work for it too. Connect a home installs it over SSH or connects with a one-time code (from Flathub, install the host yourself, then connect over SSH or with a code); in its window, sign its agents in, make projects, start tasks and talk to their agents. Expect rough edges.
- **Althar's command line** starts or joins a task on your server over SSH and shows its agent at work; leave, and it keeps going.
- **Computer use.** A task's agent uses the apps on your Mac as you would, with one permission request a turn, Take over and Stop.
- **Artifacts you talk to.** Open a design beside its own chat, point at a part and say what to change, compare boards side by side, and save it to a repository.
- **Goals.** Type `/goal` and a task's agent keeps working toward it, turn after turn, until it's done, stuck, or you pause it.
- **Slash commands** in every composer: `/goal`, `/compact` and more.
- **What's new.** Althar has a tab of its own, with what each version brings and what's coming next.
- **Browser sign-in** to GitHub, GitLab and Linear.
- **Searches and sub-agents in the thread:** what agents look up, the pages they read, the agents they hand off to, and a summary each time they compact.
- **Contributors' forks.** Work on a contributor's pull request from their fork, on GitHub or GitLab, where they allow edits from maintainers.
- **Review bots you trust,** such as CodeRabbit, Copilot or Claude's review, pass their findings to the task's agent.
- **GPT-6.2 Sol,** through Codex.
- **More agents.** Add any coding agent from the ACP registry, or your own by its command.
- **Terminals in Althar,** along the bottom of the window, each opening in a drawer.
- **Bring in work** from Conductor, Orca, T3 Code, Paradev, Cursor and Codex: their worktrees become tasks, with Claude Code's and Codex's conversations as their history.
- **Mobile builds.** Agents run Xcode, the iOS Simulator, the Android emulator and Gradle.
- **Comments on a diff's lines,** sent to the task's agent.
- **Dictate from anywhere** with a shortcut, and put the words in a task or the coordinator after.
- **ClickUp** as an issue tracker.
- **One task, several issues,** each updated as it starts and closed or commented on when it merges.
- **New projects three ways:** start empty, from a folder, or clone several repositories at once.
- **Local-only projects** merge their tasks on your computer, and go on a code host when you like.
- **Carry on after a usage limit:** a task stopped at its limit resumes by itself once it resets.
- **Tune the built-in presets:** their agent, reviewer, effort and brief, with Reset.
- **Free up space** from About Althar, which shows how much space Althar takes.
- **Your own gateway:** agents read `ANTHROPIC_BASE_URL` and `OPENAI_BASE_URL`.

## Improved

- **Althar answers at once** however busy your computer is.
- **Plan usage** shows when each limit resets, and how far through it you are.
- **Running low keeps your model:** Althar moves to another account or agent with the same model before it switches models.
- **Lighter when idle:** the coordinator sleeps after half an hour, and Home opens fast in a long-used profile.
- **Compaction while idle:** a full conversation is compacted and carries on, rather than starting over.
- **Agents share the CPU,** so none starves the rest; long threads open and scroll smoothly.
- **The coordinator knows the project** from the start of each session, and agents know your local date and time.
- **Up next** starts work as soon as an agent is free, says what a waiting task waits for and when your Mac is holding agents back, and keeps stopped work with Resume.
- **Choose a task's reviewer,** or none, in Change the team, and turn a change back into a prototype and into a change again.
- **Only required checks** hold a change back; Ship says what it will do before it does.
- **Markdown files** show their HTML as GitHub does.
- **The thread** says what happened in a short line, never an agent's raw output.
- **When git fails,** you see git's own error, and a broken worktree link is repaired.
- Claude Code's adapter is 0.113.0 and Codex's is 2.12.1.

## Fixed

- Dictation tries a failed transcription again before showing an error.
- A lost agent connection ends the turn, rather than showing Following up.
- Gemini can use Althar's tools.
- Signing in to Codex no longer fails while DNS is slow.
- An aside's permission request no longer moves the window.
- The coordinator finds a fork's pull requests, whatever its remotes are called.
- Screenshots over GitHub's 10 MB limit still reach the pull request.
- Ship shows for commits waiting before a task's first pull request.
- Run servers nothing owns are stopped, and a task's runs stop when its worktree is cleaned up.
- Board cards stay in their column while you drag the divider.
- A message sent to an idle agent no longer flashes as queued.
- Reviewing your own pull request posts on GitHub as a comment, with its verdict.
- GitHub Copilot's enterprise sign-in goes to your enterprise's host.
- A ready task's card in the thread says what the board says.
- Failing checks always reach the agent or you.
- Closing the browser beside a task brings the board back.

Tell us what breaks: Report a problem in the app, or https://github.com/AltharIDE/althar/issues
