What changed since 0.8.0.

Undo in a task: take back a message and everything after it, and the files its turns changed go back too, with the prompt back in your draft. GitHub Copilot now runs inside Althar on your own Copilot sign-in, Markdown files open rendered, and you choose whether a message sent while an agent works steers it or waits its turn.

## New

- **Undo from here.** Undo from any message you sent in a task: it and the replies after it leave the conversation, the files those turns changed go back to how they were, and the prompt returns to your draft.
- **Markdown files open rendered,** with what changed marked, and the source a click away.
- **Steer or queue.** Choose what a message sent while an agent works does: steer it now, or wait for the turn to end. Enter does the one you chose, and ⌘Enter the other.
- **How accounts take turns.** Choose how new work picks among an agent's accounts: the one with the most usage left, the one with the least, your order, at random, or always the first. Each project can choose its own.
- **Merge without queue.** Merge follows the repository's merge queue; where GitHub lets you bypass it, Merge's menu can merge directly.

## Improved

- **GitHub Copilot runs inside Althar,** on your Copilot sign-in, no longer through OpenCode.
- **A message sent mid-turn** shows in the queue at once.
- **Leads read the coordinator's conversation** with you, when their task needs what you said there.
- **Model pickers show models and effort only;** how much usage each account has left stays in Plan usage.
- **One title bar on every Linux desktop that allows it,** KDE, Xfce, Cinnamon, MATE and COSMIC among them, with the window buttons where your desktop puts them.
- **Errands' notes** say what happened in one short line, with the full answer folded under Details.
- **Closing the window remembers** whether you chose to keep work running.
- Claude Code's adapter is 0.106.0 and Codex's is 2.6.0.

## Fixed

- Moving to another account when one runs out happens quietly, and keeps the model and effort you chose.
- A turn's working time no longer counts waiting in line or its session starting.
- A thread never shows new work under an idle turn, nor a running turn while what it took still waits in the queue.
- Turning crash reports off stops the reports still waiting to be sent.
- Clicking Althar in the Dock while its last window closes opens a window.
- Asides clear when you clear them, and one you moved stays where you put it.
- A task working in your own checkout lets go of it once the task is settled.
- Dropping or joining a change cleans up its folder, on Windows too.
- Escape closes a task's menu.

Tell us what breaks: Report a problem in the app, or https://github.com/AltharIDE/althar/issues
