# The merges lab

A prototype: the coordinator's thread after the person asks it to merge ("see everything thru to main"), as it reads today and in three tidier directions, at four moments of the merges. No runtime, no Electron.

```bash
cd apps/desktop && ../../node_modules/.bin/vp dev --config lab/merges/vite.config.ts
```

At `http://localhost:6141`. The bar along the bottom, or its keys, swaps in place:

- **0, A, B, C**: today (the runtime's items through the app's own `blocksOf` and `ThreadBlocks`); A, one card in the reply; B, a track for each; C, one line at the thread's foot.
- **1–4**: just asked, some merged, one sent back, all in. **P** plays them one after another.
- **S**: four at once, or just one. **D**: dark.

`?sheet=1` draws a direction's four moments side by side, for its shots (`?d=a&s=four&sheet=1&theme=dark`).

What every direction does the same, beside its own form: an errand that merges is a row of the unit, not a line of its own; the coordinator's turns that only took a merge step draw nothing; the unit sits in the reply that asked for it, at a card's distance; once all are in, it is the kit's merge line folded to one line.
