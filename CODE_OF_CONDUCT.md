# Code of conduct

Althar's issues, pull requests, discussions and other spaces follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good faith, and keep criticism on the work, not the person. Harassment, insults and personal attacks aren't welcome, from anyone, in any form.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour concerns you, email **conduct@althar.ai**. Only the maintainers read it, and reports are kept confidential. We'll reply, look into it, and act as the Covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines) describe.
